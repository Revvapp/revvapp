Bookings & Payments

How does payment work?

When you book, your card is authorized — a hold, not a charge — for the job price. The detailer is paid once the job is marked complete and a 24-hour dispute window passes with no dispute raised. Revv takes a 10% platform fee from every job. Payments are processed by Stripe; we never see or store your full card number.

When am I actually charged?

Only after the work is finished. If a booking is cancelled or declined before the job happens, the hold is released and you are never charged. Depending on your bank, a released hold can take a few business days to disappear from your statement.

What is the dispute window?

You have 24 hours after a job is marked complete to raise a dispute if something is wrong. Our team reviews every dispute and decides between releasing the payment, refunding you in full, or issuing a partial refund. Payment is held until that decision is made.

Revv Care

What does it cover?

Revv Care is damage protection on every completed job, covering up to $2,500 in accidental damage. File a claim from your invoice within 72 hours of the job completing, with photos. Our team reviews and decides within a few business days. Revv Care is not insurance.

For Detailers

How does the subscription work?

Detailers pay $34.99/month to appear in client search and take bookings, with a free trial — 60 days for Founding Pro, 14 days otherwise. If a payment fails, the profile is temporarily hidden from search until it is resolved. You can cancel at any time.

When do I get paid?

Your payout is created automatically once the job completes and the 24-hour dispute window closes, and is sent to the bank account connected during Stripe onboarding. You receive 90% of the job price; Revv retains a 10% platform fee.

Safety & Account

How do I report a problem?

Open the booking or job in question and use Report a Problem. Reports go directly and confidentially to our trust & safety team and are never shown to the other party. If you feel unsafe, contact local emergency services first, then let us know.

How do I manage notifications?

In the app, go to Profile → Notifications to turn email and text messages on or off. Push notifications follow your device’s permission settings. We will still email you about account security and anything we are legally required to send.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile → Edit Profile → Delete Account. This removes your profile, vehicles and contact details. We retain transaction and dispute records in anonymized form where we are required to for tax, accounting and fraud-prevention purposes.

I can’t sign in

Use Forgot Password on the sign-in screen to get a reset link. If the email does not arrive, check your spam folder, then email support@revvapp.net from the address you signed up with.
